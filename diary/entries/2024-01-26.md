After the running session and dinner event with AstroRunners, I was having a space conversation with my Argentinian colleague on the train back to my home. He’s experienced both the Argentinian and Japanese space industries and working cultures, and he said it was definitely more aggressive in terms of design and verification in Argentina. Well, it’s true that they were a satellite constellation company and that itself already acts as a redundancy which means you don’t really need to have every single one of your satellites perfectly designed and tested. But still I think Astroscale is in some way a bit too conservative on the verification process. Obviously, the more you test, the higher the quality, but that improvement curve will almost flatten out at a certain point and it feels like we are pushing through in that high investment low return region. We have to follow that famous equation: 技術の向上度 = (1+a)^N where a=一回の実証での技術向上度, N=実証の頻度, (Nの向上がスピードアップのカギ). Also we definitely need to put more focus and time on the design process where the issue correction cost is the lowest. Currently, I’m like the only guy looking after the GNC algorithm design and I strongly suggest Astroscale to put more focus, time, money and resource in the design phase.
