Summmer school course description: 'Beyond Earth' - is a dynamic journey into space exploration where students delve into the critical issues around satellites, engineering behind rockets and unknowns of the universe. The workshops deliver opportunities to imagine “what if” scenarios and provide hands-on experience on engineering water bottle rockets. Ultimately, this course aims to foster critical thinking skills and a broader perspective on why we explore the frontiers.
