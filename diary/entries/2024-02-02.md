I knew some people didn't have good impression of ISAK after graduation, but today I felt that directly. As I'm involved in the alumni fundraising campaign, I asked one of my kohais if she's interested in giving back to school. She said she doesn't really want to give the money to ISAK, but not just that ISAK owes her money she feels. That moment is when I realised I had to change my strategy. Not to assume everybody likes and is willing to return the favour to the school. I think I need to emphasise the fact that the donation you are making is for your friends' performances, you don't need to put focus on the school's new building or whatever you are not interested in.
