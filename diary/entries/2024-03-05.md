Everything you do is for humanity. Why are we worth it? Why do you care about politics, a multi-planetary species? It's a good question to ask because at the foundation level, what is my philosophy and why does it lead to this conclusion? When I was a teenager, I had an existential crisis to figure out what's the meaning of life. For me at least, the religious texts and all of them I got my hands on did not seem convincing. I started reading philosophers. Be careful reading German philosophers as a teenager; it really is not going to help with your depression. I was struggling to find the meaning of life here, then I read 'Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy,' and basically what Douglas Adams was saying was that we don't really know what the right questions to ask are. They come to the conclusion that the real problem is in trying to formulate the question. The universe is the answer; what are the questions? The more we can expand the scope and scale of consciousness, the better we can understand what questions to ask about the answer that is the universe. The more we expand consciousness and become a multi-planetary species or ultimately a multi-stellar species, we have a chance of figuring out what the hell is going on. This is why I think we should have more humans, both biological and digital consciousness, and why we should become a multi-planetary and multi-stellar species so that we can understand the nature of the universe. If you accept as a proposition that we don't really understand the meaning of life and we wish to understand the meaning of life, then in order to understand the meaning of life, we should expand consciousness such that we can ask better questions, learn more, expand beyond the solar system, ensure that life on Earth is good collectively for civilization, and we can be less dumb about the nature of the universe and maybe we can answer some of the questions about where it came from and where it's going. I think it's a sound philosophy; it's the least unsound philosophy that I can think of. - Elon Musk
